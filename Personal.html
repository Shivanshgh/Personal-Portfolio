01 · The Journey

The journey so far

I'm an Electronics & Communication Engineering student who ended up spending most of his free time writing code instead of drawing circuits. Somewhere between coursework and late-night side projects, I found that I liked building things more than just studying how they work.

These days that means experimenting with AI/ML, shipping small web tools, and picking apart problems in hackathons until they turn into working software. I like projects with a clear before-and-after — something broken, or missing, that now exists because I built it.

I'm still early in this — second year, plenty left to learn — but the direction is clear: software, AI, and the things you can build when you combine them.
